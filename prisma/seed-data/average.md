# Citizen Water Testing on the Kessel River

> Synthetic proposal written for the Proposal Evaluator demo. All people, organisations,
> places and figures are fictional. Intended quality level: **average**.

## Applicant and organisation

Jordan Pike, Coordinator, Friends of the Kessel River. Friends of the Kessel River is a
volunteer group founded in 2019 that organises river clean-ups and nature walks.

## Funding requested

- **Amount requested:** $60,000
- **Project duration:** 12 months

## Summary

We want to set up a group of local volunteers to test the water in the Kessel River regularly,
because the official testing does not happen often enough. We will share the results online
so residents know when it is safe to swim.

## Problem and need

People in our area are worried about pollution in the river. Some swimmers got sick last
summer, and there are farms upstream that may cause run-off. The Water Authority only tests
the river a few times a year, so nobody really knows how clean it is most of the time.
Volunteer monitoring has worked in other places.

## Objectives

1. Recruit around 30 volunteers.
2. Test the river regularly at about 10 locations.
3. Put the results on a website.

## Approach and work plan

We will advertise for volunteers through our mailing list and local newspaper. Volunteers
will get a training session on how to use water test kits. They will then test the water at
sites along the river, probably every few weeks, and send us the results. We will check the
results and put them on a website with a map. We may ask the Water Authority to compare some
of our results with theirs.

## Team and capacity

Jordan Pike has run Friends of the Kessel River for five years and has organised many
volunteer events. We have about 200 people on our mailing list. We do not yet have a
scientist on the team but plan to find an adviser.

## Risks and mitigation

Volunteers might stop taking part, so we will try to keep them motivated with regular
meet-ups. Bad weather could stop some testing.

## Measuring success

We will count how many tests are done and how many people visit the website.
